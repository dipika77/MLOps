# Acme Corp - Employee HR Policy (2026)

## 1. Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Employees are
expected to work 40 hours per week. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 AM to
4:00 PM, during which everyone should be available for meetings.

## 2. Remote Work Policy

Employees can work from home up to 3 days per week after completing their
probation period of 3 months. Remote work must be approved by the reporting
manager on the HR portal at least one day in advance. Employees working
remotely must be reachable on Slack during core hours.

Fully remote arrangements are only allowed for roles marked "Remote" in the
offer letter.

## 3. Leave Policy

- **Annual (paid) leave:** 24 days per calendar year. Leave accrues at 2 days per month.
- **Sick leave:** 10 days per year. A medical certificate is required for more than 2 consecutive sick days.
- **Casual leave:** 6 days per year, cannot be carried forward.
- **Parental leave:** 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver.
- **Carry forward:** A maximum of 10 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next year.

Leave requests must be submitted on the HR portal. Requests of more than 5 days
must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.

## 4. Public Holidays

Acme Corp observes 12 public holidays per year. The holiday calendar is
published on the HR portal every December for the next year.

## 5. Expense Reimbursement

- Travel, meals during business trips and client meetings are reimbursable.
- Meal allowance during travel is capped at $50 per day.
- Receipts must be uploaded within 30 days of the expense.
- Reimbursements are processed with the monthly payroll.
- Any single expense above $500 needs pre-approval from the department head.

## 6. Learning and Development

Every full-time employee gets an annual learning budget of $1,000 for courses,
certifications and books. Unused budget does not carry forward.

## 7. Performance Reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Salary revisions
are announced in January based on the December review.

## 8. Contact

For HR questions email hr@acme-corp.example or raise a request on the HR portal.
