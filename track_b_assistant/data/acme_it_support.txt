ACME CORP - IT SUPPORT GUIDE

1. PASSWORD POLICY
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and contain uppercase letters,
lowercase letters, numbers and a special character. Passwords expire every 90
days and the last 5 passwords cannot be reused. After 5 failed login attempts
the account is locked for 15 minutes.

2. RESETTING YOUR PASSWORD
Go to https://sso.acme-corp.example/reset and enter your work email. You will
receive a one-time code on your registered phone. If you do not have access to
your phone, raise an IT support ticket with priority "high".

3. VPN ACCESS
All employees must connect to the company VPN (Acme SecureConnect) when
accessing internal tools from outside the office. Install the SecureConnect
client from the Self Service portal. VPN sessions disconnect automatically
after 10 hours.

4. LAPTOP AND HARDWARE
New employees get a laptop on their first day. Laptops are replaced every 3
years. If a laptop is damaged, report it within 24 hours by raising a support
ticket. Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately so that IT can
remotely wipe the device.

5. SOFTWARE INSTALLATION
Only approved software from the Self Service portal can be installed. To request
new software, raise a ticket with the software name and the business reason.
Approval usually takes 2 business days.

6. SUPPORT TICKET PRIORITIES
- low: general questions, response within 3 business days
- medium: issue affects work but a workaround exists, response within 1 business day
- high: cannot work at all (for example account locked, laptop not booting), response within 4 hours

7. IT HELPDESK CONTACT
Helpdesk hours: Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
Email: it-help@acme-corp.example
Phone extension: 4357 (HELP)
