# Acme CloudDrive - Product FAQ

## What is Acme CloudDrive?

Acme CloudDrive is a secure cloud storage and file sharing product for small and
medium businesses. It supports file sync across Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS and
Android.

## What plans are available?

| Plan       | Price (per user / month) | Storage        | Max file size |
|------------|--------------------------|----------------|---------------|
| Free       | $0                       | 5 GB           | 100 MB        |
| Starter    | $6                       | 1 TB           | 5 GB          |
| Business   | $15                      | 5 TB           | 50 GB         |
| Enterprise | Contact sales            | Unlimited      | 250 GB        |

Annual billing gives a 20% discount on Starter and Business plans.

## Is there a free trial?

Yes. The Business plan has a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required.

## How is my data protected?

All files are encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Business
and Enterprise plans support two-factor authentication (2FA) enforcement and
single sign-on (SSO) with SAML 2.0.

## Where is the data stored?

Customers can choose a data region: US (Virginia), EU (Frankfurt) or India
(Mumbai). The region cannot be changed after the account is created.

## How long are deleted files kept?

Deleted files stay in the trash for 30 days on Free and Starter plans, and for
180 days on Business and Enterprise plans.

## What is the refund policy?

Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime but are not refunded. Annual plans can
be refunded on a pro-rata basis within the first 30 days.

## What is the uptime SLA?

Business plan: 99.9% monthly uptime. Enterprise plan: 99.99% monthly uptime
with service credits if the SLA is missed.

## How do I contact support?

- Free plan: community forum only.
- Starter: email support, response within 24 hours.
- Business: email and chat support, response within 4 hours.
- Enterprise: 24/7 phone support with a dedicated account manager.
